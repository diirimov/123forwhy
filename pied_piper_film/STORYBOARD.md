**How the music shaped the story.** The brief's timestamps were moved to the real musical events:

- The soundtrack's dark turn at 24.7 s (B♭, gong, half-time E♭, then a silence) is where the **rats drown**.

- The town's refusal to pay plays over the soured return of the hook at 31.8 s. The flute's "wrong note" bend lands as the chest slams.
- The final drop at 40.6 s is the Piper's return at night.
- The four full-band silences (12.13, 40.37, 47.54, 56.03 s) are the four moments where the film holds its breath.
