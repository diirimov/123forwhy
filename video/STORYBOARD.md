# "3 Scariest Diseases of the Middle Ages" — Beat-Synced Storyboard

Format: 1080×1920 vertical, 60 fps, 90.000 s, H.264 + AAC. Audio = the approved master WAV, untouched (no added SFX, no edits).
Grid: 128 BPM · beat 0.469 s · bar 1.875 s · step(1/16) 0.117 s. "B5.3" = bar 5, 16th-step 3.

**Through-line:** a single bell-tower town lives through three waves of fear. Every shot is driven by the groove: walking/running characters step on beats, lights and cameras punch on kicks, and the hook's 3-3-2 rhythm (steps 0/3/6) is the "slam" pattern for doors, stamps and title words. Each disease has its own colour world: **Black Death = blood-red night**, **Leprosy = cold teal mist**, **Sweating Sickness = feverish amber**; the finale unites them in a parchment book.

Brief timings were snapped to musical boundaries: Hook 0–7.5 · Black Death 7.5–30 · Leprosy 30–52.5 · Sweating Sickness 52.5–75 · Finale 75–90.

## Shot cards (camera · light · text · sync)

- **Global:** camera never fully static (drift/arc during reads), 1–3 % zoom punch on every kick in drop sections, impact shake on section hits, parallax layers (sky / far skyline / street / foreground props), fog bands for depth, pre-blurred far layers as depth of field, sub-frame motion blur on whips and slams, film grain + vignette + paper texture for one coherent look. Text stays in the TikTok safe area (x 90–990, y 260–1400).
- **Text style:** disease names in blackletter (Pirata One) with ink outline + coloured glow; statements in Cinzel Black capitals with dark plates; book in IM Fell English. Every reveal lands on a musical accent and is held ≥ 1.2 s for reading.
- **Transitions** are physical: doors/shutters, gate, page turns, camera dives into shadow, hard cuts on the three silence gaps (14.77, 44.77, 74.53 s).

**Main risk:** procedural 2D art looking cheap. Test: render character rig + street stills before the full build and judge silhouette, palette, depth.
